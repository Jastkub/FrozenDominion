Music
=====

The boss fights and the citadel are original compositions for Frozen Dominion
(Jastkub). No one else's music is used. Their scores are in tools/music/ - one
script a piece - and tools/music/render_all.py plays them into this folder.

citadel_theme.ogg       the citadel, while it is explored
                        "Halls of the Frozen Dominion"
turnkey_fight.ogg       the Turnkey             "The Last Lock"
priestess_fight.ogg     the Rime Priestess      "Litany of Rime"
lamplighter_fight.ogg   the Lamplighter         "Last Light"
shepherd_fight.ogg      the Shade Shepherd      "Dance of Shades"
overseer_fight.ogg      the Forge Overseer      "The Cold Forge"
bone_lord_fight.ogg     the Bone Lord           "Over the Chasm"
drowned_fight.ogg       the Drowned Lady        "Beneath the Black Ice"
aurochs_fight.ogg       the Ice Aurochs         "Stampede"
frosty_fight.ogg        Frosty                  "The Hunt of the Alpha"
monstrosity_fight.ogg   the Ice Monstrosity     "The Colossus Wakes"

Instruments: performed with the samples of the Versilian Studios Chamber
Orchestra 2 Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE),
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) - no attribution required; named here
as the instruments the music is played on.

velkhar_phase1.ogg, velkhar_phase2.ogg, velkhar_phase3.ogg, velkhar_outro.ogg
and silent_winter.ogg (the music disc) are not made by those scripts.
