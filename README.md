# Frozen Dominion

**A soulslike citadel for Minecraft** — Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1.

Somewhere on the snowy plains the snow stops melting. Above ground there is a ruin, a gate and a tower; underneath,
five stages of halls — the Prisons, the Crypts, the Court, the Storm and the Depths — eight of the king's court still at
their posts, a prisoner chained beneath them, and at the far end, on a throne of ice, **Velkhar, the Hollow Sovereign**.

## What is in it

- A four-phase final boss: the Bulwark King, the Stormblade, the Twin Blades and the Hollow Magus, whose finale is
  fought above the clouds.
- Eight minibosses — the king's court — each with its own hall, rules, entrance and death scenes, and voice.
- The Ice Monstrosity, a second boss you can later wake, tame and ride.
- A citadel built as a journey: keys that know their doors, runes behind false walls, traps, waking statues, chained
  chests, Frost Shrines that bring you back with your things, and a parkour over a chasm of spikes.
- The Frost Anvil, Everfrost, Kingsrime and four legendary weapons forged from what the court leaves behind.
- The Chronicle of the Citadel — an in-game guide (with Patchouli) in English and Polish.

## Requirements

GeckoLib and Curios. Optional: Patchouli (the Chronicle), Player Animator (body animations), Better Combat, JEI,
Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

## Building

`./gradlew build` — Java 17 for 1.20.1, Java 21 for 1.21.1. The `tools/` folder holds the Python scripts the citadel,
models, textures, sounds and the guide are generated with.
